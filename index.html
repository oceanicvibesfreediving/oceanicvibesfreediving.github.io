Professional Instruction

This is not a snorkeling tour.

At OceanicVibes, we train athletes, watermen, and dedicated beginners to safely and comfortably unlock their true aquatic potential. We specialize in advanced depth progression and professional AIDA certification.

Whether you are mastering the Frenzel maneuver, working on Mouthfill equalization for deep dives, or overcoming mental barriers through deep relaxation and CO2 tolerance training, our methodology ensures elite-level safety and measurable progression.

We utilize the unparalleled conditions of the Riviera Maya—from the endless blue off the coast of Playa del Carmen to the crystal-clear, mystical depths of dozens of surrounding Cenotes, and the stunning waters of Bacalar.
